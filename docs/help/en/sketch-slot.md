# Slot

Draws a straight slot: two semicircles joined by tangent lines.

**Sketch toolbar:** **Slot**

```figure
image: ../images/sketch-slot-steps.svg
alt: The end-arc centers and slot radius
caption: 1 and 2 — semicircle centers; 3 sets the radius. The full slot width is twice the radius.
width: 600
```

## Steps {#steps}

1. Click **Slot**.
2. Click the center of the first semicircle.
3. Click the center of the second semicircle.
4. Click to set the slot width (the distance from the axis to the cursor is the radius).
5. Optionally, type the radius in the [quick dimension](sketch#quick-dimensions) field and press `Enter`.

## What is created {#result}

- Two arcs of the same radius (an equal constraint) and two lines tangent to them.
- A construction axis between the arc centers. Set its length and position with [dimensions](sketch-dimensions).
- Centers placed on existing points get coincident constraints.
