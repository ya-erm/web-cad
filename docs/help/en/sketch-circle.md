# Circle

| Tool | Shortcut | Defined by |
|---|---|---|
| **Center point circle** | `C` | The center and a point on the circle. |
| **3 point circle** | — | Three points on the circle. |

## Steps {#steps}

**Center point circle:**

1. Click **Center point circle** (`C`).
2. Click the center.
3. Click a point on the circle (or type the diameter in the [quick dimension](sketch#quick-dimensions) field and press `Enter`).

**3 point circle:**

1. Click **3 point circle**.
2. Click three points the circle should pass through.

## Details {#details}

- A center placed on a sketch point coincides with it; a center placed at the origin is fixed there.
- If the point on the circle (or any of the three points) is placed on an existing point, that point lies on the circle (a point-on-curve coincident constraint).
- After the circle is created, a **diameter** field is offered.
- Drag a circle by its edge to change the radius, or by its center to move it.
