# Point

Places standalone sketch points. Points are most often used as [hole](hole) centers, as reference points for [lofts](loft) and [planes](plane), or for dimensions.

**Sketch toolbar:** **Point** · **Shortcut:** `Shift+S` (in sketch mode)

## Steps {#steps}

1. Click **Point** (`Shift+S`).
2. Click to place points. The tool stays active until you press `Esc`.

## Details {#details}

- A point placed on a curve, a line midpoint or an existing point gets the matching constraint; a point placed at the origin is fixed there.
- Position points with [dimensions](sketch-dimensions) to other points or lines.
- In the [Hole](hole) feature, sketch points are selected as hole locations; circle centers work too — select the circular edge.
